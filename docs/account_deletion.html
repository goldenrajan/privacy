Account and Data Deletion

Zentury POS

If you'd like to delete your Zentury POS account and the data associated with it, you can request this at any time.

1. How to Request Deletion

Send an email to zenturytechora@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My Account", from the email address you used to sign up (or the Google account you signed in with).

Please include the shop/business name registered in the app so we can locate your account.

2. What Gets Deleted

Once we verify your request, we will delete:

  • Your account (email/password or Google sign-in credentials)
  • All business data synced to our servers under your account - products, sales, customers, expenses, and related records

3. What Doesn't Get Deleted Automatically

Data stored only on your device is not affected by this request, since we never have access to it. You can remove that at any time by uninstalling the app.

4. Timeframe

We will complete deletion requests within 30 days and confirm by email once done.

For any other questions about your data, see our Privacy Policy or contact zenturytechora@gmail.com.

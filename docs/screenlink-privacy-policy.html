1.3 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

ScreenLink stores the following information only on your device (never sent to our servers):

  • Device IDs of helpers you've connected with
  • Last connection date and session count

Session History:

  • Room codes from past sessions
  • Helper names
  • Session start/end times and duration
  • Session notes (if you add them)

  • Limited to last 50 sessions

App Settings:

  • Voice prompts enabled/disabled
  • Notification blur enabled/disabled

Session Recordings (Optional):

  • If you enable recording, videos are saved to your device's local storage
  • Recordings are never uploaded to our servers
  • You have full control to view, share, or delete recordings

2. How Screen Sharing Works (Peer-to-Peer)

This is the most important part of our privacy approach.

2.1 Direct Connection

When you share your screen:

  1. Your device creates a direct, encrypted connection to the helper's device using WebRTC technology
  2. Screen content and audio are transmitted peer-to-peer (device-to-device)
  3. Our server is NOT involved in transmitting your screen or audio

2.2 What Our Signaling Server Does

Our signaling server (required for WebRTC) only:

  • Generates random 6-digit room codes
  • Relays connection setup messages (like a phone operator connecting a call)
  • Notifies when someone joins or leaves a session
  • Automatically expires rooms after 1 hour of inactivity

Our server does NOT:

  • Store room codes after sessions end
  • See your screen content
  • Record your voice or audio
  • Log personal information
  • Store session metadata (duration, participants, etc.)
  • Track who you connect with

2.3 Data Encryption

All peer-to-peer communication is encrypted using:

  • DTLS (Datagram Transport Layer Security) for data channels
  • SRTP (Secure Real-time Transport Protocol) for audio/video

This is the same encryption used by video conferencing services like Google Meet and Zoom.

  • Contact Google for data deletion requests: Google Support

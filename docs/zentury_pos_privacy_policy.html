Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 11, 2026

Zentury Techora ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Zentury POS mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when using the App:

  • Business Information: Shop name, owner name, address, phone number, email, GST number
  • Product Information: Product names, prices, stock quantities, categories, barcodes, SKUs
  • Customer Information: Customer names, phone numbers, email addresses, purchase history
  • Supplier Information: Supplier names, contact details, purchase orders
  • Transaction Data: Sales records, expenses, payment information
  • Account Credentials: PIN/password for app security, Google account for backup (optional)

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you use the App, we may automatically collect:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: App features used, crash reports, performance data
  • Analytics Data: Anonymous usage statistics through Firebase Analytics

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Core Functionality: To provide POS features including inventory management, billing, sales tracking, and customer management
  • Data Backup: To backup your business data to your personal Google Drive (when enabled)
  • App Improvement: To analyze usage patterns and improve app performance
  • Technical Support: To diagnose and fix technical issues
  • Security: To protect against unauthorized access and fraud
  • Analytics: To understand how users interact with the App and improve user experience

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local Storage

All your business data (products, sales, customers, etc.) is stored locally on your device using encrypted SQLite database. This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

3.2 Cloud Backup (Optional)

If you choose to enable cloud backup:

  • Your data is backed up to your personal Google Drive account
  • Backup files are encrypted and stored in your private Google Drive storage
  • We do not have access to your backup data
  • You can disable backup at any time

3.3 Security Measures

We implement appropriate security measures including:

  • Local data encryption
  • PIN/password protection
  • Secure authentication for Google Drive access
  • Regular security updates
Important: While we strive to protect your data, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

4.1 Google Services

  • Google Drive API: For optional cloud backup of your data (requires your explicit consent)
  • Google Sign-In: For authentication and backup access
  • Firebase Analytics: For anonymous usage analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics: For crash reporting and app stability

4.2 Google AdMob

The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob, which may collect:

  • Device identifiers for ad personalization
  • Ad interaction data
  • IP address and general location

You can opt-out of personalized ads in your device settings. Learn more at Google Privacy Policy.

4.3 Third-Party Privacy Policies

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy Policy
  • AdMob Privacy Policy

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following circumstances:

  • With Your Consent: When you explicitly authorize us to share information
  • Service Providers: With third-party service providers (Google, Firebase) necessary for app functionality
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government regulation
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets
  • Protection: To protect our rights, property, or safety, and that of our users

6. Your Data Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: All your data is accessible within the App
  • Modification: You can edit or update your data at any time
  • Deletion: You can delete individual records or all data by uninstalling the App
  • Export: You can backup and export your data to your Google Drive
  • Portability: Your backup files can be downloaded from your Google Drive

7. Data Retention

We retain your information as follows:

  • Local Data: Stored on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App
  • Backup Data: Stored in your Google Drive until you delete it
  • Analytics Data: Anonymous analytics data is retained according to Firebase's retention policies (typically 2-14 months)
  • Crash Reports: Retained for 90 days to help us improve app stability

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for business use and is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us immediately.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data is stored locally on your device. If you use cloud backup, your data is stored in Google Drive's data centers, which may be located in various countries. Google complies with applicable data protection laws and regulations.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy in the App
  • Updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy
  • Providing an in-app notification for material changes

Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Permissions Used

The App requests the following permissions:

  • Internet Access: For Google Drive backup, analytics, and ads
  • Network State: To check connectivity before backup operations
  • Camera: For barcode scanning (optional feature)
  • Storage: To save and read backup files
  • Google Drive Access: To backup your data (requires explicit consent)
  • Notifications: To alert you about low stock, backup status, etc.
